Frequently asked questions

How do I get started?

Create an account, add a payout account, then fund your wallet or submit a gift card from your dashboard.

How long do gift cards take?

Cards are reviewed manually. Most are processed within the time shown in your dashboard.

Can I install it as an app?

Yes. Open DFQ Exchange in your phone's browser and choose Add to Home Screen, or tap the install prompt.

Where can I get help?

Open a support ticket from your dashboard, or email support@dfqexchange.com.ng.
